The methodology

Decisions are made in space, so we coach space.

01

Scan

Teach players what to look at, when, and how often, so the picture is already in their head when the ball comes.

02

Read the space

A shared language for the spaces around the opposition (A, B, C, D, behind and in front of their lines), so coach and player talk about the same thing.

03

Choose and measure

Every pass is judged on the space it found, against the spaces that were there. Not just whether it arrived, but whether it was the right idea.

Built from years of coaching and match analysis, then tested on real games, real players and real video.
